Partition the common named low positions into t groups of width L0+1 and the others of width L0. Since q_groups K<=n<(q_groups+1)K, all group widths lie in [K,2K). In any one round every axis supplies the SAME current group width. No round mixes column widths and no nonintegral chunk is assumed.

The layout sends these existing named coordinates from axis-major to group-major order; it does not add coordinates or pad volume.
